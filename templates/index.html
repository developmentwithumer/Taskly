Features

Everything you need to
do your best work

We stripped away the clutter and kept only what actually helps you stay productive, designed with care and absolute simplicity.

Visual Kanban Board

Easily drag and drop your tasks across four smart columns: To Do, In Progress, Completed, and Overdue. Everything updates instantly across your devices so you never miss a beat.

Deep Focus Mode

Block out distractions with our built-in 25 minute focus timer. The clean, fullscreen view helps you zone in on one task at a time, checking it off automatically when you are done.

Smart Workflow

Tasks scheduled for today automatically move to your In Progress column. Any missed deadlines are clearly marked as Overdue, meaning less manual updates for you.

Lightning Fast Search

Find exactly what you need as soon as you start typing. Filter your entire board by title, status, or date without ever waiting for the page to reload.
